About

A planner who stays close to the work front.

I started as a Junior Planner in 2017. Since then I have planned turnarounds and shutdowns for refinery units at CPCL Manali and for Shell GTL in Qatar.

Most days that means building and updating schedules in Primavera P6, measuring progress against the baseline, preparing 3-, 7- and 14-day look-aheads, and running coordination meetings with execution teams, clients, procurement and safety. When work slips, I prepare the recovery plan.

What I'm looking for

A professional, work-driven team where I can apply my planning knowledge and skills, keep growing as an engineer, and help the organization meet its goals.
